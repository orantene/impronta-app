The house

An imprint, not an impression.

Impronta is a Tulum-born talent agency. We scout, develop, and place faces for the world's most demanding brands, photographers, and houses, in English and Spanish, across Mexico and Europe.

Our approach

Curated, not catalogued.

We keep a deliberately small board so every face gets real development and every client gets the right cast. From first call to final frame, bookings are handled by people who answer fast and travel light.

“Presence over polish. Always.”
