## DECISIONS & OPEN RISKS (post-adversarial verification, 2026-06-05)

Three adversarial reviews stress-tested this plan against the live source. All three surfaced real defects; their corrections are now folded into the waves below. The decisions:

### D1 — Context split (GATE-C): **NOT on the critical path. Decision pre-made: do NOT split unless a number forces it; expected trigger probability ≈ nil.**
- The one real pro-split wedge is verified: `executeBuilderNodeOperation` (`edit-context.tsx:4201-4282`) lists `selectedBuilderNodeId`+`selectedSectionId` in its deps (`:4279-4280`) **only** to stamp an audit annotation (`:4262-4263`), and **54** call-sites reference `executeBuilderNodeOperation` — so every selection change recreates all of those action callbacks → a fresh `value` identity, which would leak selection-churn back into action-only consumers even after CW-3 moved selection to a store. That genuinely threatens GATE-C's premise ("nothing left to churn on").
- **But it is closed by the exact ref-pattern the file already uses, NOT by the structural split.** `commitBuilderTreeMutation` already reads the tree via `builderTreeRef.current` (`:4143`) → deps only `[capHistory, queueRouterRefresh]` (`:4198`). Refs `pageVersionRef` (`:2104`) + `builderTreeRef` (`:2107`) exist for exactly this. Selection is simply not yet mirrored. **The fix (now W2-T4a): add `selectedSectionIdRef`/`selectedBuilderNodeIdRef` (~4 lines), read them in the audit annotation, drop the two from `executeBuilderNodeOperation`'s deps.** Same risk class as CW-1/2/3 — not the 122-field/38-consumer/autosave-undo-CAS-coupled refactor. This is what makes CW-3 actually deliver selection-quiet action consumers.
- GATE-C stays in the plan **as written**, but its expected trigger probability is lowered to ≈ nil. **If GATE-B ever returns NO-GO, prefer `React.memo` on the ~10 cheap leaf consumers** (shortcut-overlay, theme/assets/collections drawers, edit-shell toast/save-chip leaves) over the `EditActionsContext`/`EditStateContext` decomposition. The full split is the deferred tail (§6), not a planned wave. Full write-up: `web/docs/marathon-findings/adversarial-context-split-necessity.md`.

### D2 — Seatbelt path defect: **BLOCKER fixed.** The Wave-0 React-rendering tests were mistargeted and would have silently NOT run.
- Verified: `vitest.config.mts` sets `test.include = ["test/**/*.test.tsx"]` — **only files under `web/test/` are collected** (there are 0 `.test.tsx` files under `web/src`). The plan originally prescribed the three load-bearing provider seatbelts (W0-T2/T3/T4) at `web/src/components/edit-chrome/*.test.tsx` → they would be **silently skipped by vitest** and have no jsdom under `tsx --test`. Net effect: dead test files, green CI, false confidence — re-enacting the exact orphaned-test bug Wave 0 is meant to fix. **All three seatbelts are now retargeted to `web/test/components/edit-chrome/*.test.tsx`; W0-T2 EXTENDS the existing file; a new Wave-0 exit gate (W0-T8) proves each test actually executes (must FAIL when its assertions are reverted).**

### D3 — W0-T1 orphan-set wording corrected (no false "safe set").

### TOP 3 RISKS
1. **Silently-skipped seatbelts (D2).** If an agent follows literal `src/`-rooted test paths, the provider/undo/render-tax safety net never runs and Wave 1/2/3 surgery on the 6,437-LOC `edit-context.tsx` proceeds unguarded. **Mitigation: W0-T8 exit gate (revert-must-fail proof) + retargeted paths; Wave 1 is BLOCKED until W0-T8 is green.**

## 0. Reframes that govern every decision (non-negotiable)

1. **Profile before you cut.** No context-split surgery until the Wave-0 profiling run confirms residual churn after the cheap wins. The goal is **"no whole-editor re-renders," NOT "split the context."**
2. **Seatbelt before surgery.** Provider-render tests + the orphaned-test CI wiring land in Wave 0, before any edit-context.tsx or render.tsx mutation.
3. **Classes-don't-publish is a TRUST bug, not a capability gap** — it ships in Wave 1, not Wave 5. The product actively invites an action (the Classes tab tells you to create a class) and then silently discards it on publish.
4. **Don't add Capability before Feel.** Capability (86) is already the highest dimension; custom breakpoints / visual pickers / crop polish are Wave 5, gated behind Fast + Feel.
5. **Undo/redo + save-conflict recovery are the soul** of "feels like a program." They are Wave 1 (trust) and Wave 3 (feel), not deferred.
6. **One writer per shared-core file.** `edit-context.tsx` (6,437 LOC), `render.tsx`, and `style-panel.tsx` (9,630 LOC) are single-writer files. Tasks that touch them SERIALIZE; everything else fans out.

## 3. PROFILING-DRIVEN GO/NO-GO FRAMEWORK (context split, "Sub-step E")

The audit's #1 unresolved item is whether the monolithic `edit-context.tsx` needs a structural context split. **We do NOT split on principle.** The decision is metric-gated against the Wave-0 (W0-T7) baseline and re-measured after the cheap wins.

### The cheap wins (always do these, in order)
1. **W2-T1** — canvas options memo (fixes the canvas tree, independent of the provider).
2. **W2-T3** — hover micro-store (removes the #1 churn source from the value-memo deps).
3. **W2-T4a** — **selection-ref fix** (the ~4-line edit; drops selection from `executeBuilderNodeOperation`'s deps so its 54 dependents stop churning on selection — this is what makes step 4 quiet action-only consumers, and is the verified reason the structural split is not needed).
4. **W2-T4** — `dirty`/`selectedSectionId` micro-store (removes the click / first-of-burst churn).

These are cheap because the pattern is already shipped (`client-builder-canvas-bridge.ts`) and the action callbacks are already `useCallback`-stable. **W2-T4a is the load-bearing addition** surfaced by the adversarial review: without it, moving `selectedSectionId` to a store still leaves selection-churn leaking through the action callbacks, which would have re-opened the split case. With it, an action-only consumer has nothing left to churn on.

### GATE A — after W2-T1 + W2-T3 (measured on the same I1/I2/I4/I5 interactions)

Re-run the profiler. Evaluate against these thresholds (per-interaction, median of 5):

| Signal | Source | GO (stop — split NOT justified) | NO-GO (continue to W2-T4, then re-gate) |
