# Inviting your models to claim their profiles

One page. Follow it in order. Start with five people, not fifty.

---

## Before you start

Have the **phone numbers** of five models you can reach on WhatsApp today. Not
their email. Phone.

The email addresses already in the system were typed when the profiles were
created and most of them are not real, so email will not reach anyone. The link
you copy below works no matter what the email says.

---

## For each model

1. Open **Roster** and click the model.
2. Find the card that says **"You own this profile, talent has no account yet"**.
3. Click **Send claim invite**.
4. Type their **phone number**. Leave the email box **empty**.
5. Click **Send invite**.
6. A **Claim link** appears. Click **Copy**.
7. Send it to them on WhatsApp with the message below.

If they lose the link, open the same card and click **Resend invite**. That
gives you a fresh link and cancels the old one.

---

## What to send them

**Spanish**

> Hola {nombre}, soy Alejandra de Impronta. Te armé tu perfil y ya está listo.
> Con este link lo reclamás y podés editar vos misma tus fotos, tu bio y tus
> tarifas: {link}
>
> Cualquier duda escribime por acá.

**English**

> Hi {name}, this is Alejandra from Impronta. I set up your profile and it is
> ready. Use this link to claim it, then you can edit your own photos, bio and
> rates: {link}
>
> Message me here if anything is unclear.

---

## What happens on their side

They open the link, create a password, and the profile becomes theirs. Your
photos, bio and rates stay exactly as you left them. Nothing is lost. From then
on they can edit their own profile and you keep working on it too.

---

## Stop after five

Do five, then wait until at least one person has actually finished claiming.
If something is wrong with the flow we want to find it on five people, not
forty six. Tell whoever set this up once the first one goes through.

---

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| No Claim link appears after sending | The invite was not created. Try again, and if it fails twice, report it. |
| They say the link does not open | Send them the link again from **Resend invite**. Links expire. |
| They already have an account | They can still claim. Ask them to sign in first, then open the link. |
| The button does nothing | Report it. Do not keep clicking. |

---

## Why we are doing this

Right now 87 of our 92 profiles have nobody attached to them. That means no one
can update their own photos or rates, and every change has to go through you.
Once a model claims her profile she maintains it herself, and the profile stops
going stale the moment you stop touching it.
