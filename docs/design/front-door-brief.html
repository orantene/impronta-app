Replacement · The Brief

One conversation.
It becomes the order.

A stranger asks, picks lines, gets one offer, holds a time and pays. No account to start. The chat never keeps its own prices. The same engine that runs the point of sale writes every line.

  1. 1
    Ask with no accountThe first message sends. Email is only “Save this conversation”, and on Jorg’s site it never says Tulala.
  2. 2
    Lines, not a cartA talent, a package, a ticket or a service becomes a line on one draft order. Send request sends the words and the lines together.
  3. 3
    One offerAccept, ask for a change, or decline. A revision replaces the card. It does not stack.
  4. 4
    Then time, then payThe slot is held. Pay sits in the thread and flips to Paid. Each trade asks for its own facts: a chef asks how many guests, a massage asks the session length, a lash artist asks the day and the hour.
